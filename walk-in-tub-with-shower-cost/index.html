What does a walk-in tub with shower cost?

Aging Safely Baths publishes one installed range for walk-in tubs: $15,250 to $20,000 after current promotions. That is the tub, the shower setup you choose and professional installation by factory-certified installers, usually finished in one day. Where a model lands inside that range depends on its size, door, therapy options and how much of the bathroom has to change around it.

Cost articles online often quote a single number for "a walk-in tub" and then mention the shower as an upgrade. That makes comparisons hard, because one company's base price may include the hand shower and wall surround while another lists them later. The fix is simple: ask every company for the same itemized list, which is what the rest of this page builds.

If the combo is for a veteran, the VA's HISA grant may help when the VA finds the change medically necessary. The lifetime limit is $6,800 for a service-connected condition or $2,000 for other conditions, and it does not pay for spa, hot tub or jacuzzi features such as jets.

Which choices move the price the most?

  1. Tub size and door. Wider, longer, bariatric and transfer-style tubs sit at the top of the range. Check the fit first with our 60-inch alcove fit checker.
  2. Therapy systems. Air jets, water jets and heated seats add hardware and often a circuit. Many households that want a combo for showering skip jets entirely.
  3. How much wall gets covered. A surround to full shower height covers more area than a tub-height splash panel.
  4. Plumbing moves. A tub that drains on the other end, or a valve moved to a new wall, adds labor. Our plumbing checklist lists what usually stays and what changes.
  5. Hot water. If the household needs a bath and showers in the same hour, a larger or faster heater may be part of the project. Run the numbers with the water heater check.
  6. Hidden repairs. Soft subfloor or old water damage only shows up when the old tub comes out. Ask how it would be priced before you sign.

Is one combo cheaper than a tub plus a separate shower?

Usually, yes, because a combo uses one alcove, one drain and one installation day. A separate roll-in shower is a second project. Using Aging Safely Baths' published installed prices, here is how the three common plans compare:

Installed price ranges for three bathroom plans
PlanInstalled rangeBest when
Walk-in tub with shower$15,250 to $20,000One alcove, one person soaks and another showers standing
Roll-in shower only$13,500 to $17,000Nobody needs a soak, or a wheelchair or shower chair is in the picture
Walk-in tub plus separate roll-in shower$28,750 to $37,000 (two projects added together)Room for two fixtures and very different needs in the house

The combo is not automatically the right answer. If nobody in the house actually soaks, a roll-in or walk-in shower is simpler and costs less. If a parent uses a wheelchair, a combo still asks them to step through a door. Our combo vs shower comparison covers those cases, and Aging Safely Baths shows the walk-in tub shower combo models it installs.

Can you add a shower to a walk-in tub you already own?

Often, yes. Many walk-in tub faucets already include a hand shower; Ella's Bubbles five-piece fast-fill faucet, for example, has a hand shower limited to 1.9 gallons per minute. Turning that into a full standing shower usually means adding three things:

  • A slide bar or a riser with a fixed head, mounted into solid backing.
  • Waterproof wall panels above the tub deck, at least where the spray lands.
  • A curtain rod or glass screen, with the liner hanging inside the tub rim so water drains back into the tub.

Two cautions. First, spray aimed at the door can wear on the seal, so plan the curtain or screen before the shower head. Second, the person showering is standing on the tub floor behind a door ledge, so grab bars and a nonslip floor matter. Our walk-in tub shower safety guide has a checklist for both bathers.

Questions people ask about cost

How much does a walk-in tub with shower cost?

With Aging Safely Baths, a walk-in tub with shower costs $15,250 to $20,000 installed after current promotions, done in one day in most homes. Size, door style, therapy options and any plumbing or water heater changes decide where a model lands in that range.

Does adding a shower to a walk-in tub cost extra?

The shower parts are real line items: a hand shower on a slide bar, often a fixed head, a valve and diverter, a waterproof wall surround and a curtain or glass screen. Ask for each to be listed as included or priced, so quotes from different companies compare fairly.

Is a walk-in tub with shower cheaper than a tub plus a separate shower?

Usually. A combo uses one alcove and one installation. Using Aging Safely Baths published prices, a combo is $15,250 to $20,000 installed, while a walk-in tub plus a separate roll-in shower adds up to $28,750 to $37,000.

Will I need a bigger water heater for a walk-in tub with shower?

Sometimes. A full bath plus showers in the same hour can outrun a small tank. Compare the hot water you need with the first-hour rating on your heater, or ask the installer to check it before you order.

Does the VA HISA grant cover a walk-in tub with shower?

It may, when the VA finds the change medically necessary. The HISA lifetime limit is $6,800 for a service-connected condition or $2,000 for other conditions, and HISA does not pay for spa, hot tub or jacuzzi features such as jets.
